First days in Germany. Arrived in Munich on the 27th to get situated in Germany before heading up to Berlin by train on the 30th to get my time at the Freie Universität started. Munich was incredibly beautiful, most of these pictures are from there. I had a great time and met some interesting people in Munich. Berlin so far has been such a stark difference from any city in the US. The architecture, the style of buildings, and the culture are so different from what I am used to. I start classes at the University tomorrow, so I am looking forwards to doing that and getting there. I will probably make weekly updates/posts like this going forwards. The culture shock of some of the things that are normal here that aren't in America is also been quite interesting, but those are stories for another time.
